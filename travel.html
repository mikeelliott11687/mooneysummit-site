Recommended Arrival and Departure Procedures

VFR arrivals and departures are highly recommended, weather permitting, to facilitate a safe and efficient flow into and out of Peter O Knight Airport. The procedures in the graphics below are provided to assist you with remaining clear of the Tampa Class B and multiple Class D airports in the immediate area of KTPF. Following these suggested procedures will also make us all predictable and help us concentrate our visual lookout on the places where we expect to see others as they approach and depart KTPF, and to work our own sequencing headed inbound from the east.

Arrival

Please review the arrival graphics for the details on VFR arrivals from the north and south. These procedures will keep you clear of the Class B 1,200’ shelf for as long as possible before turning inbound to KTPF. Ensure you’ve descended to 1,000’ prior to making the turn west from the I-75 bridge over the Alafia River. Items needing particular attention are:

  1. Tampa Class B altitude restrictions
  2. MacDill Air Force Base Class D proximity to KTPF
  3. 1667’ MSL TV towers 4 miles east of the I-75 bridge turn point inbound to KTPF
  4. Runways 04 and 36 are RIGHT TRAFFIC

Departure

The departure procedures are quite simple and designed to get you clear of the Class B 1,200’ and 3,000’ shelves as expeditiously as possible. An immediate turn to 090 after departing any runway will get you in a position to climb as soon as possible and remain clear of the surrounding Class D airspace. Again, ensure you are out from under the 3,000’ shelf prior to climbing out of 2,500’ on course. If you wish to fly home under IFR, please plan to depart VFR and pick up your clearance with Tampa Approach on 120.475 once airborne and on course, weather permitting.

Use extra caution departing Rwy 22: MacDill AFB Class D is 0.6 mi off the departure end.
