Three days in Tampa

Ramp, classroom, and a night out.

Fly into Peter O. Knight on Davis Islands, spend two days learning from people who know the airplane, and finish with dinner on the river.

Thursday, Sept 23

Airport Day

Peter O. Knight Airport (KTPF), hosted by Atlas Aviation. Exhibitors, activities, seminars, and time on the ramp with other Mooney owners. The day closes with a BBQ.

Also on Thursday: a Tampa International Airport (TPA) air traffic control tower tour, limited to the first 8. Tour time and meeting point will be emailed to participants.

Sign up for the TPA tower tour when you register (limited to the first 8)

Friday, Sept 24

Safety Day 1

Tampa International Airport conference center, next to Airside F. Industry safety speakers and Mooney experts. Participants can earn FAA WINGS knowledge credits at the Basic, Advanced, and Master levels.

Saturday, Sept 25

Safety Day 2 and Banquet

More Safety Day sessions at the TPA conference center, then the evening banquet, silent auction, and raffle at American Social, on the river downtown.

Full Summit XII details

Why pilots come back

Education that fits your airplane.

The Summit was founded in 2014 to make good Mooney pilots better. The speakers know the airframe, the systems, and the habits that keep Mooney pilots safe. It is not a generic safety day.

  • Earn FAA WINGS knowledge credits at all three WINGS levels: Basic, Advanced, and Master
  • Mooney experts and industry safety speakers
  • An Airport Day with exhibitors, services, and your own airplane on the ramp
  • Run by volunteers. No directors, administrators, or speakers are paid.

How the safety seminar works

The Bill Gilliland Foundation

When a Mooney family has the worst day of their life, we show up.

The Bill Gilliland Foundation is a program of Mooney Summit, Inc. It reaches out to the immediate families of downed Mooney pilots with personal contact and real financial help. Summit proceeds and your gifts make it possible.
