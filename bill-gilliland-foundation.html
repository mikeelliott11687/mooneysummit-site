Bill Gilliland Foundation

Mooney pilot family crisis fund

The Bill Gilliland Foundation is a program of Mooney Summit, Inc., a 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 47-4343940). After Mooney pilot Bill Gilliland died on July 11, 2014, the Summit community built this fund to help Mooney families at the moment they need it most.

Your tax-deductible gift supports emergency crisis outreach and financial assistance for the immediate families of downed Mooney pilots.

What your gift does

  • Funds the Foundation’s initial outreach to Mooney families after a fatal or serious accident
  • Helps underwrite the initial family check (currently $1,200) sent with first contact
  • Supports additional aid case by case when the board authorizes it
  • Is used for Foundation family help (Summit event costs are separate)

Why this fund exists

Most aviation nonprofits focus on training or scholarships. This fund is different: it is a Mooney community crisis response for families, with personal outreach and tangible help, not a cold form letter alone.

How to donate

PayPal: use the Donate button below.

Zelle: send to Iwannadonate@mooneysummit.com (registered to Mooney Summit, Inc.)

Donate by email or Presidents Club: for gifts of $1,000 or more, or to ask about the Presidents Club, email Iwannadonate@mooneysummit.com

Questions about assistance: families and referring pilots, see Foundation Assistance, or email outreach@mooneysummit.com
