Presidents' Club

For Mooney pilots who give generously.

A tax-deductible contribution of $1,000 or more to Mooney Summit, Inc., a 501(c)(3) charity, includes annual membership in the Presidents' Club.

Why join

Members recognize the value of the Bill Gilliland Foundation in helping downed Mooney pilots' families in their time of greatest need, with crisis intervention and financial assistance.
