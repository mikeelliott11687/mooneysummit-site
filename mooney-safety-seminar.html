Mooney safety seminar

WINGS-credited safety education, built for Mooney pilots.

Mooney Summit is the annual type-specific safety education event for Mooney pilots. Mooney Summit, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 47-4343940) and a FAASTeam Industry Member. Each Summit pairs an open Airport Day with Safety Day seminars where participants earn FAA WINGS knowledge credits.

What you get

  • Type-specific Mooney safety education from instructors and industry speakers who know the airplane
  • Participants can earn FAA WINGS knowledge credits at all three WINGS levels: Basic, Advanced, and Master, in Safety Day sessions. WINGS details will be posted when the Summit XII agenda is final.
  • Airport Day exhibits, activities, and community time with other Mooney owners
  • A banquet and silent auction that help fund crisis aid for families of downed Mooney pilots through the Bill Gilliland Foundation

Who it is for

Mooney owners, pilots transitioning into a Mooney, instructors, and industry partners who want structured safety education and a well-run annual gathering.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mooney Summit an FAA WINGS safety seminar?

Participants can earn FAA WINGS knowledge credits at all three WINGS levels: Basic, Advanced, and Master. Mooney Summit, Inc. is a FAASTeam Industry Member. Participation in the FAASTeam does not constitute FAA endorsement of any organization, product or service.

When is the next Mooney Summit?

Summit XII is September 23 to 25, 2027, in Tampa, Florida.

How is this different from other Mooney training events?

The Summit is a nonprofit, volunteer-run community safety event with an Airport Day, two Safety Days, and a banquet whose proceeds support Mooney family crisis aid.

Where do I find the WINGS activity numbers?

They will be posted here once the Summit XII sessions are listed on FAASafety.gov.
