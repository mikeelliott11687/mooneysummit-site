Mooney Summit XII

September 23 to 25, 2027. Tampa, Florida.

One Airport Day, two Safety Days, and our traditional banquet and silent auction. Plan to arrive Wednesday, September 22, and fly home Sunday, September 26, to get the most out of it.

Mark your calendar

Mooney Summit XII comes to Tampa, September 23 to 25, 2027. Fly your Mooney into Peter O. Knight Airport on Davis Islands, learn from Mooney experts at Tampa International Airport, and finish with dinner on the river downtown.

The plan

Agenda and speakers coming soon.

Thursday, Sept 23

Airport Day

Peter O. Knight Airport (KTPF), hosted by Atlas Aviation. Exhibitors, activities, seminars, and time on the ramp with other Mooney owners. The day closes with a BBQ.

Also on Thursday: a Tampa International Airport (TPA) air traffic control tower tour, limited to the first 8. Tour time and meeting point will be emailed to participants.

Sign up for the TPA tower tour when you register (limited to the first 8)

Friday, Sept 24

Safety Day 1

Tampa International Airport conference center, next to Airside F. Industry safety speakers and Mooney experts. Participants can earn FAA WINGS knowledge credits at the Basic, Advanced, and Master levels.

Saturday, Sept 25

Safety Day 2 and Banquet

More Safety Day sessions at the TPA conference center, then the evening banquet, silent auction, and raffle at American Social, on the river downtown.

Questions

Is registration open, and what does it cost?

Yes, registration is open now. Early bird registration is $199 per person through July 31, 2027, then $249. Each pilot registration includes one non-pilot spouse or significant other at no additional fee. Register on RegFox.

Can I earn WINGS credit?

Participants can earn FAA WINGS knowledge credits at all three WINGS levels: Basic, Advanced, and Master. WINGS details will be posted when the Summit XII agenda is final. Participation in the FAASTeam does not constitute FAA endorsement of any organization, product or service.

Can my spouse or family come?

Yes. The Summit has always welcomed families.

Where do I park my airplane?

Plan to fly into Peter O. Knight Airport (KTPF), where Atlas Aviation is our host.
