Where it goes

Individual and corporate gifts fund the Summit's safety education. Funds above the cost of the event support the Bill Gilliland Foundation, which provides crisis assistance to the families of downed Mooney pilots.

Want your gift to go only to the Foundation? Say so in the PayPal note or email Iwannadonate@mooneysummit.com.
