Sponsors and exhibitors

Reach the pilots who fly Mooneys.

Our sponsors and exhibitors make the Summit possible, and they keep the registration fee low. In return, you get a room full of engaged Mooney owners.

Become a Summit XII sponsor

Sponsor a meal, exhibit on Airport Day, present a session, or donate to the silent auction or raffle. Every sponsorship supports a 501(c)(3) safety mission, and proceeds above cost go to the Bill Gilliland Foundation.

Summit XII sponsorship levels and benefits are coming soon. Email us to talk about how your company can take part.

Email sponsorship@mooneysummit.com
